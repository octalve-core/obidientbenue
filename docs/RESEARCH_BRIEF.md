## Verified identity and 2027 candidates

- Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) is listed as a registered political party by INEC. Its public brand uses a blue victory-sign icon, red `NDC` letters, and `Service to the People`. Source: <https://www.inecnigeria.org/parties/nigeria-democratic-congress>.
- Peter Obi is reported on INEC's September 2026 final 2027 presidential list under NDC, with Rabiu Musa Kwankwaso as running mate. Sources: <https://www.thecable.ng/tinubu-atiku-obi-sowore-inec-clears-18-presidential-candidates-for-2027-poll/> and <https://www.ndcnigeria.com/>.
- The 2027 NDC Benue governorship ticket lists Prof. Terhemba Shija and Dr Raymond Edoh. Sources: <https://ngelections.com/blog/2027-governorship-final-lists-by-state/> and <https://benue.today/dave-ogbole-appointed-deputy-director-general-of-shija-edoh-campaign-organisation/>. Check the primary INEC final list PDF before a production campaign launch.
- Candidate portraits are included from a campaign banner provided directly for this project. The small logo is extracted from that same client-provided banner. Verify that the 2026 INEC-recognised artwork matches the approved current campaign logo.

## Values vs enforceable commitments

- The NDC's own public site discusses production-oriented economic transformation, security, youth opportunity, government accountability, and transparent procurement: <https://ndcnigeria.com/>.
- The Obidient Movement's public site describes transparency, competence, youth empowerment, and citizen participation: <https://www.obidients.com/>.
- Peter Obi's reported August 2026 campaign launch discussed the production economy, local enterprise, investment and human capital: <https://von.gov.ng/peter-obi-launches-2027-presidential-campaign/>.
- The original full 2027 manifesto document has not been independently retrieved and verified for this project. Do not publish new specific numerical promises, timelines or funding allocations as candidate commitments without an original document approved by the campaign. Do not relabel a 2023 programme as the 2027 manifesto.

## Local Benue context (not promised outcomes)

- A Save the Children/Plan International 2024 needs assessment covering 3,659 surveyed households reported food, education, health and water as major priorities. The figures refer to that survey sample and time period, not a universal 2026 state census: <https://resourcecentre.savethechildren.net/document/multi-sectoral-needs-assessment-benue-state-nigeria-april-2024>.
- Policy discussion sections may therefore cover security, farming, education, basic health, services, and transparent governance, but must not claim Shija/Edoh have endorsed the website's proposed wording unless documented.

## Polling-unit directory and privacy

- INEC's official polling-unit locator is <https://cvr.inecnigeria.org/pu>. INEC's national directories report 176,846 polling units and 8,809 wards; Benue has 23 LGAs, 276 wards and 5,102 polling units in INEC reporting.
- A public GitHub project has transcribed an INEC portal snapshot from 10 August 2026; `scripts/import-inec.mjs` pins that source and rejects records unless Benue totals and code integrity reconcile. This is **secondary transcription**, not an official INEC CSV download. Obtain official confirmation and sample direct PU verification before enabling form submissions.
- Political opinion/affiliation is sensitive personal data under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. A named data controller, contact channel, retention and deletion policy, consent, security and restricted access are preconditions to accepting registrations: <https://ndpc.gov.ng/faqs/>.

## Publishability rules

1. Cite every candidate appointment or public promise with its original, date-appropriate source.
2. Never invent testimonials, public support numbers, results, impact metrics, partner endorsements or donation totals.
3. Public registration is not INEC registration, a PVC service, or proof of formal NDC party membership.
4. Keep optional features unlinked and inaccessible until their actual data, permissions and user journeys are certified.
5. Obtain campaign editorial approval for official wording, contact information and use of logos and candidate imagery.
